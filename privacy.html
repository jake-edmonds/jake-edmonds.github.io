Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Google API client is used only by its owner, to connect the owner's self-hosted Home Assistant installation to the owner's own Google account.

  • Data accessed: Google Drive files created by Home Assistant (its backups) and the owner's Nest devices. Access is granted only by the owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use: the data is used only by the owner's Home Assistant, which runs on the owner's own hardware. No data is sent to the author of this page or any other party.
  • Sharing: no data is collected, sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Retention: nothing is stored outside the owner's own Home Assistant and Google account.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
